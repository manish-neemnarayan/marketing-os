# Phase 1: ICP & Audience Reality Repository

## 1.1 Audience Profiling & Calibration
- **Operational Profile:** Founders, Product/Engineering Leads, and Solo Operators (1–25 person teams) building digital products or services.
- **Generational & Cultural Calibration:** High-contrast structured frameworks, zero corporate fluff, concrete numbers, architecture diagrams, and direct peer-to-peer engineering/operator candor.
- **Internal Vocabulary:** CAC, churn, LTV, cold start, pipeline leak, context switching, manual ops, unit economics, shipping velocity, burnout, scope creep.
