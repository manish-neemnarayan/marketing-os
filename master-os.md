## Phase 1: Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) & Audience Reality
- **Operational Profile:** Exact job titles, revenue benchmarks, team size, daily responsibilities, and industry niche.
- **Generational & Cultural Context:**
  - *Digital Natives / Gen Z:* Fast visual pacing, meme fluency, self-deprecating or witty tone, raw smartphone aesthetics, low tolerance for corporate jargon.
  - *B2B / Senior Decision-Makers:* Clean typography, high-contrast layouts, structured frameworks, data-backed claims, and industry-standard shorthand.
- **Internal Vocabulary:** Exact words, terms, and acronyms used in real conversations (e.g., CAC, churn rate, client onboarding friction, pipeline leaks, burnout).
- **ICP Problem Matrix:** Every piece of content traces back to one row (Tension Category | Underlying Emotion | Verbatim Customer Complaint | Content Angle).

## Phase 2: Goal Definition & Metric Mapping Framework
| Funnel Stage | Business Goal | Primary Metrics | Secondary Metrics | Core Business Question |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Awareness (TOFU)** | Reach & brand discovery | Unique Reach (% non-followers), Total Impressions, Follower Growth Rate | Video Completion Rate, Share of Voice (SOV) | *Are new, relevant prospective customers discovering the brand?* |
| **Engagement (MOFU)** | Resonance & community trust | Engagement Rate (by Reach), Saves & Bookmarks, Shares / Reposts | Meaningful Comments, Inbound DMs | *Does the content hold attention and compel audience interaction?* |
| **Traffic & Intent (MOFU/BOFU)** | Mid-funnel evaluation | Link-in-Bio Clicks, CTR, Referral Sessions (via UTMs) | Profile Visits, Landing Page Bounce Rate | *Is social driving qualified users onto owned digital channels?* |
| **Conversion (BOFU)** | Revenue & acquisition | Attributed Leads / Sign-ups, Direct Sales Volume, CPA | ROAS, Checkout Initiations | *What is the direct, measurable financial return generated?* |
| **Brand Health** | Reputation & retention | Net Sentiment Score, Support Resolution Time, Unsolicited UGC | Community Activity, Repeat Purchases | *How do customers genuinely perceive and advocate for the brand?* |

## Phase 3: The Multi-Stream Ideation Engine
- **Stream 3A (Internal ICP Pain Points):** Pull directly from `context/icp-matrix.md`.
- **Stream 3B (Competitor Gap Analysis — Anti-Copy Filter):** Isolate top 5% outliers, reverse-engineer whether performance came from topic, hook angle, or paid distribution, and target unanswered questions in comments with a 10x deeper version.
- **Stream 3C (Cross-Industry Transposition — "Borrow the Shell, Change the Soul"):**
  1. *The Mechanic:* Identify the structural device driving retention in an unrelated industry.
  2. *The Deconstruction:* Strip away the product/industry to expose the psychological formula.
  3. *The ICP Translation:* Re-skin the formula using our brand aesthetic codes, voice, and ICP reality.
- **The 4 Guardrails for External Inspiration:**
  1. *Aesthetic Integrity:* Never adopt a foreign visual style that clashes with brand codes.
  2. *Value Alignment:* Calibrate copy to match the emotional weight of the product.
  3. *Price-to-Effort Calibration:* Tailor CTA and value proposition to the investment level required.
  4. *Originality Defense:* Ensure the original source is unrecognizable.

## Phase 4: Format-to-Goal Deployment & Pre-Production Gate
- **4.1 Multi-Format Capability Matrix:**
  - *Short-Form Video (Reels/Shorts/TikTok):* Awareness = fast hooks/contrarian takes; Engagement = face-to-camera debate/AMAs; Conversion = rapid before/after proof & screen walkthroughs.
  - *Carousels (Multi-Slide):* Awareness = shareable data dumps/cheat sheets; Engagement = step-by-step masterclasses/teardowns built for saves; Conversion = Problem-Agitation-Solution ending with pitch.
  - *Single Static Graphics / Infographics:* Awareness = high-contrast pattern interrupts; Engagement = "Choose A or B" prompts; Conversion = direct release banners/offers.
  - *Text-First Content (LinkedIn/Threads/X):* Awareness = contrarian observations/frameworks; Engagement = transparent failure/tactical breakdowns; Conversion = problem-agitation leading to lead magnet/trial.
- **4.2 The 10-Second Pre-Production Greenlight Gate:**
  1. *The Specificity Gate:* Name the exact individual in the ICP who stops scrolling.
  2. *The Payoff Gate:* State the exact value received within the first 10 seconds or 2 slides.

## Phase 5: Creative Deconstruction (The "Why")
1. **The First 3 Seconds (Hook Anatomy):** Verbal/written hook + visual motion/contrast/prop.
2. **Core Messaging & Narrative Structure:** Immediate follow-through without preamble; clear archetype (tactical breakdown, transformation, critique, or solution).
3. **Tone & Delivery:** Authoritative, peer-to-peer, satirical, or urgent.
4. **Visual Packaging & Typography:** Hierarchy, safe zones, contrast, studio vs lo-fi aesthetic, cut intervals.
5. **CTA Proportionality:** Match the ask to the depth of value delivered (e.g., Save for checklists, Link click for case studies).

## Phase 6: Deep Performance Analysis
Evaluate across: (1) Content & Format Drivers, (2) Audience Demographics & Distribution Verification, (3) Competitive Gap Analysis, and (4) Sentiment & Social Listening (feed new objections back into Phase 1).

## Phase 7: Strategic Synthesis & Continuous Feedback Loop
1. **Double Down:** Pick top 2 creative formats and hook archetypes that beat KPIs.
2. **Eliminate / Pivot:** Retire or re-engineer formats/angles that repeatedly missed their assigned metric.
3. **Draft Sprint Hypotheses:** Document 1–2 testable adjustments in `context/sprint-hypotheses.md`.
